UK–Nepal Business Forum 2026

Building Stronger Partnerships, Creating Shared Prosperity

26 November 2026 Kathmandu Marriott Hotel, Kathmandu, Nepal

Organised by NBCCI in joint partnership with the British Embassy Kathmandu.

About

A serious conversation, designed to travel beyond the room.

The UK–Nepal Business Forum 2026 is the flagship bilateral platform for advancing trade, investment and strategic partnerships between the United Kingdom and Nepal. It brings together senior government representatives, business leaders, investors, academics and development partners to strengthen economic cooperation and unlock opportunities for collaboration.

The Forum is designed as annual bilateral engagement aligned with the UK’s Modern Industrial Strategy and Trade Strategy, creating business-to-business connections, commercial outcomes and a practical pathway for UK expertise and investment to support Nepal’s economic growth.

One DayFor honest exchange, useful introductions and decisions with a next step.
Two NationsA shared platform for the UK and Nepal's public and private sectors.

Panel Discussion I

Strengthening UK–Nepal Economic Partnerships: Advancing Trade, Investment and Private Sector Growth

To explore how UK-Nepal expertise and investment partnerships can unlock new opportunities for trade, strengthen private sector competitiveness, attract sustainable investment, and drive long-term economic growth in both countries.

Moderator

Dr. Nicholas Gribit

First Secretary, Trade

DBIST South Asia

Nicholas Gribit has been First Secretary of Trade, India, at the UK’s Department for Business and Trade since July 2025. He is based in Bengaluru, with responsibility for the UK’s trade promotion work in Advanced Manufacturing, as well as oversight of the UK’s trade portfolio in Nepal. Prior to his appointment, he has worked for the UK Government in various international and trade-related roles, most recently as head of trade policy for South Asia in the Department for Business and Trade.

Panel Members

Ms. Yanki Ukyab

CEO

Investment Board of Nepal

Yanki Ukyab is an economist with 20 years of experience across the private and public sectors, including senior roles with the Government of Nepal, the United Nations and the private sector across the US, Nepal, the Pacific and Southeast Asia.

She took office as Chief Executive Officer of the Investment Board of Nepal (IBN) in August 2026. IBN is Nepal’s apex institution for facilitating major private investment, foreign direct investment and public-private partnerships.

Prior to her current appointment, Ukyab held senior positions with the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), the UN Resident Coordinator (UNRCO) system and the Government of Nepal, including as Country Economist for UNDP Nepal and in senior economic roles in the Pacific. She previously served as Lead of the Economic Analytics Unit at the Investment Board of Nepal, working on major investment and infrastructure initiatives.

Ukyab holds a master’s degree in public policy from the University of Oxford and a master’s degree in economics from the University of Missouri. Her career has centered on economics, investment and development, with experience spanning government, international development and the private sector.

A truly dynamic professional who thrives in fast-paced environments, Bradley values the diverse challenges of the ECA and wider financial landscape. He brings more than four years of professional experience, primarily within Whitehall, alongside roles in the third sector and other independent ventures.

Steve retired from the Diplomatic Service in 2015 and became Chair of the Anglo-Thai Society in 2017. He also Chairs the UK’s largest Thai Charity - Safe Child Thailand. Currently paying his first visit to Nepal and has been the British Nepal Chamber of Commerce’s CEO since January 2025.

Mr. Manoj Paudel

Manoj Paudel is the Founder and Chair of Aadhyanta Fund Management. His work spans private capital, enterprise development and economic policy in Nepal. He previously chaired the Investment and International Affairs Committee at the Federation of Nepalese Chambers of Commerce and Industry (FNCCI) and currently serves on its Executive Committee. He has advised government ministries, national institutions and senior policymakers on investment, infrastructure and economic policy, and has served on the boards of public companies. He holds an MSc from the London School of Economics and was a Chevening Scholar.

Dr. Kedar Prasad Acharya is a higher education professional currently serving as Director at the University Grants Commission (UGC), Nepal, in the Planning, Monitoring and Evaluation Division. His professional and research interests include strategic management of HEIs, higher education financing, institutional autonomy, governance and leadership, human resource management, quality assurance, and the internationalization of higher education. His research particularly focuses on strengthening strategic management practices and institutional performance in HEIs.

With his professional experience in higher education policy and institutional management, he is interested in promoting international academic partnerships, collaborative research, knowledge exchange, and stronger linkages between higher education, innovation, and the evolving needs of the labour market.

With over 20 years’ experience at the British Council, Reesha has led large-scale, multi- stakeholder education programmes that strengthen higher and technical education systems, support international research collaboration and skills development. Powering Nepal's Energy Future: Applying UK Science, AI and Innovation

To examine how UK expertise in science, AI, and smart infrastructure can help accelerate Nepal's energy transformation through grid modernisation, digitalisation, climate resilience, and innovative energy solutions.

Moderator

Mr. Bipin Basnet

Country Lead

ASI Nepal

Bipin Basnet is a Country Lead for Adam Smith International (ASI), a UK Based Global Advisory company working in Nepal since 1997, where he leads and supports programmes and advisory work in Nepal spanning economic development, governance reform, energy and infrastructure, private sector development, and investment. He previously worked at Investment Board Nepal, where he was involved in the structuring and development of major infrastructure and hydropower investments, including work related to project due diligence, market engagement, foreign direct investment and public-private partnerships. His broader experience includes advising on Nepal’s energy and investment landscape and supporting engagement between government, development partners and the private sector. Bipin has a particular interest in mobilising investment and applying innovation and international expertise to support Nepal’s sustainable and resilient economic development.

Kieron is a hydraulic and mechanical engineer and began his career as a hydro turbine designer.

As the Founder and Managing Director of Hydroplan UK, he is responsible for the company’s commercial operations while providing strategic and technical and project management of hydropower projects. He has led major multi megawatt hydropower portfolios, energy systems and complex grid integration projects, including providing due diligence for lenders and equity investors.

A former chairman and CEO of the British Hydropower Association (BHA), Kieron is a current board member of the BHA and an advocate of the hydropower industry for over 30 years.

In line with the firm’s strategy to facilitate the development of sustainable future outcomes, he has a focus on works that lead to positive climate impacts, improved resilience and net- zero solutions based on commercially sound projects that achieve these aims. As a professional engineer he has particular knowledge and expertise for the design and implementation of renewable energy and low carbon molecular fuel systems including onshore and offshore wind farms as well as solar and hydrogen facilities and is currently involved with advisory services and the detailed design works for a number of projects being undertaken in Hong Kong, Japan, Korea and Taiwan.
