UK–Nepal Business Forum 2026

Building Stronger Partnerships, Creating Shared Prosperity

26 November 2026 Kathmandu Marriott Hotel, Kathmandu, Nepal

Organised by NBCCI in joint partnership with the British Embassy Kathmandu.

About

A serious conversation, designed to travel beyond the room.

The UK–Nepal Business Forum 2026 is the flagship bilateral platform for advancing trade, investment and strategic partnerships between the United Kingdom and Nepal. It brings together senior government representatives, business leaders, investors, academics and development partners to strengthen economic cooperation and unlock opportunities for collaboration.

The Forum is designed as annual bilateral engagement aligned with the UK’s Modern Industrial Strategy and Trade Strategy, creating business-to-business connections, commercial outcomes and a practical pathway for UK expertise and investment to support Nepal’s economic growth.

One DayFor honest exchange, useful introductions and decisions with a next step.
Two NationsA shared platform for the UK and Nepal's public and private sectors.

Panel Discussion III

Powering Nepal's Energy Future: Applying UK Science, AI and Innovation

To examine how UK expertise in science, AI, and smart infrastructure can help accelerate Nepal's energy transformation through grid modernisation, digitalisation, climate resilience, and innovative energy solutions.

Moderator

Mr. Bipin Basnet

Country Lead

Adam Smith International, Nepal

Bipin Basnet is a Country Lead for Adam Smith International (ASI), a UK Based Global Advisory company working in Nepal since 1997, where he leads and supports programmes and advisory work in Nepal spanning economic development, governance reform, energy and infrastructure, private sector development, and investment. He previously worked at Investment Board Nepal, where he was involved in the structuring and development of major infrastructure and hydropower investments, including work related to project due diligence, market engagement, foreign direct investment and public-private partnerships. His broader experience includes advising on Nepal’s energy and investment landscape and supporting engagement between government, development partners and the private sector. Bipin has a particular interest in mobilising investment and applying innovation and international expertise to support Nepal’s sustainable and resilient economic development.

Panel Members

Mr. Nav Raj Ojha

Deputy Managing Director, Transmission Directorate

Nepal Electricity Authority

Mr. Nav Raj Ojha, Deputy Managing Director (Transmission Directorate) of the Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA), holds a Master’s degree in Power System Engineering from the Institute of Engineering, Pulchowk Campus, Tribhuvan University. He has nearly 30 years of extensive experience in Nepal's energy sector, having served at the NEA since November 1997. Throughout his distinguished career, Mr. Ojha has acquired deep expertise across power generation, high-voltage transmission lines and substations (132 kV, 220 kV, and 400 kV), distribution network expansion, power trading, and system loss reduction.

Prior to his current role leading the Transmission Directorate, he held executive positions including Deputy Managing Director of the Business Development Directorate and the Distribution and Consumer Services Directorate, as well as Chief of both the Bagmati and Lumbini Provincial Offices. He has also directed key infrastructure initiatives, including major transmission line projects, smart metering, and underground distribution projects. Under his leadership, both the Lumbini (2023) and Bagmati (2024) Provincial Offices were honored with letters of appreciation by NEA Management for outstanding achievements in annual loss reduction and power supply reliability. He is a registered engineer of Nepal Engineering Council and Life Member of Nepal Engineer’s Association as well as Society of Electrical Engineer’s Association Nepal (SEEN).

Mr. Pratik Man Singh Pradhan

Vice President

Butwal Power Company

Pratik Pradhan is a hydropower specialist with more than 34 years of experience in hydropower construction, contracting, design, development and business analysis. Currently, he is the Vice-President for Business & Project Development, at Butwal Power Company (BPC) and is also the Deputy General Manager at SCIG International Nepal, a joint venture company of BPC for the development of more than 600 MW Marsyangdi Cascade Projects in Nepal.

Throughout his career, he has gained construction and contracting experiences of building dams, intakes and tunnels; research and development applications within the hydraulics, sediment handling and environment; project and business development including investment decisions and financial analysis of hydropower projects. Many of his key experiences have been in difficult and challenging mountain sites.

He has Civil Engineering Bachelor’s degree from India, a Master’s degree in Hydropower Planning and Development from the NTNU Norway, and a second Master’s degree in Civil and Environmental Engineering from the University of Michigan, USA.

Mr. Kieron Hanson

Director

Hydroplan Group UK

Kieron is a hydraulic and mechanical engineer and began his career as a hydro turbine designer.

As the Founder and Managing Director of Hydroplan UK, he is responsible for the company’s commercial operations while providing strategic and technical and project management of hydropower projects. He has led major multi megawatt hydropower portfolios, energy systems and complex grid integration projects, including providing due diligence for lenders and equity investors.

A former chairman and CEO of the British Hydropower Association (BHA), Kieron is a current board member of the BHA and an advocate of the hydropower industry for over 30 years.

Kieron’s relationship with Nepal extends over 20 years and he is a regular traveller to Kathmandu where the company has an office with a team of engineers and specialists.

Mr. Peter Thompson

Director and East Asia Energy Business Leader

Arup UK

Peter Thompson is a Director and the East Asia Energy Business Leader for Arup based in Hong Kong. He has more than 38 years of experience in the management of the design and implementation of major energy and infrastructure works projects across a wide variety of overseas countries.

In line with the firm’s strategy to facilitate the development of sustainable future outcomes, he has a focus on works that lead to positive climate impacts, improved resilience and net- zero solutions based on commercially sound projects that achieve these aims. As a professional engineer he has particular knowledge and expertise for the design and implementation of renewable energy and low carbon molecular fuel systems including onshore and offshore wind farms as well as solar and hydrogen facilities and is currently involved with advisory services and the detailed design works for a number of projects being undertaken in Hong Kong, Japan, Korea and Taiwan.

He is a Fellow of the Institution of Civil Engineers, the Hong Kong Institution of Engineers and the Energy Institute.

She serves as Chairperson of Union Hydropower Limited and Change Nepal, and as Director of Dudhkoshi Nepal Hydropower Project (95.7 MW), Union Mewa Hydro Ltd. (24.4 MW), Aayu Malun Hydropower Ltd. (21 MW), Puwakhola–One Hydropower Ltd. (4 MW), and High Himalaya Hydro Construction Pvt. Ltd.

She also represents the private sector as an Alternate Board Director at MCA–Nepal, serves as Director at NELIS Nepal, and acts as Executive Counsellor to the Republic of Armenia in Nepal, reflecting her strong international and cross-cultural engagement.

Her expertise spans strategic finance, equity raising, corporate governance, and sustainable infrastructure development.
