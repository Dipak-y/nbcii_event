The forum / 01

A serious conversation, designed to travel beyond the room.

The UK–Nepal Business Forum 2026 is the flagship bilateral platform for advancing trade, investment and strategic partnerships between the United Kingdom and Nepal. It brings together senior government representatives, business leaders, investors, academics and development partners to strengthen economic cooperation and unlock opportunities for collaboration.

The Forum is designed as annual bilateral engagement aligned with the UK’s Modern Industrial Strategy and Trade Strategy, creating business-to-business connections, commercial outcomes and a practical pathway for UK expertise and investment to support Nepal’s economic growth.

One DayFor honest exchange, useful introductions and decisions with a next step.
Two nationsA shared platform for the UK and Nepal's public and private sectors.

The measure / 07

What the Forum aims to deliver

The Forum is designed to create visible momentum: stronger dialogue, useful introductions, new institutional collaborations and a follow-up mechanism that carries progress beyond the day.

01 / Strategic

Stronger UK–Nepal bilateral trade and investment dialogue, and deeper public-private collaboration.

02 / Business

New trade and investment leads, B2B partnerships and stronger market linkages.

03 / Institutional

New collaborations across government, academia, industry and development partners.

A pipeline for future trade missions and sector initiatives, with a follow-up mechanism to track progress after the Forum.
